## 4프레임 재생성

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit attached four-frame masked grey chaser sprite sheet. Preserve character identity, hair, mask, clothing, tiny knife, detailed soft pixel RPG style, camera facing right, transparency and equal size. Repair ONLY walk poses into a genuine alternating gait, 2x2 grid reading order: 1 NEAR leg forward planted to screen right, FAR leg extended back left; 2 NEAR leg supporting vertically under hip, FAR leg passing forward with bent knee, lifted boot under hip (not a high kick); 3 FAR leg forward planted screen right, NEAR leg stretched back screen left, visibly reverse knee overlap and occlusion compared with frame 1; 4 FAR leg supporting vertically under hip, NEAR leg passing forward bent knee and lifted boot under hip. Frames 1 and 3 MUST NOT duplicate leg stance: swap which anatomical leg is forward, with far leg behind near leg at hip. Frames 2 and 4 must show opposite boot lifting. Short modest grounded steps, upright torso, subtle opposite arm swing, same knife hand, no running, jumping, kicking. Same character at all frames, consistent scale and cell-relative foot baseline; feet/hair/knife fully within each quadrant with generous transparent margin, no text, labels, ground shadows, extra characters. Actual transparent alpha. This is a game walk loop, prioritize anatomical leg alternation over four stylish illustrations.

New RIGHT sprite: SCREEN RIGHT-rooted leg supports the body vertically, SCREEN LEFT-rooted leg lifted and passing in front with visible bent knee and boot under pelvis. Natural small walk, no kicks; consistent head and feet baseline, all hair/knife/boots visible with ample margins. Keep face, mask, hair, cloth and knife identity.
