# Combat refresh

- Fire arrows retain their existing hit, explosion and ground damage. Owning fire multiplies the player's attack interval by 1/0.7, reducing firing frequency by 30%. Haste, relics and evolutions still apply multiplicatively. Fairy and turret timers are unchanged.
- Added five lower-floor encounter combinations and four upper-floor formations. Enemy count, health scaling, entry deployment and the upper support cap remain unchanged. Flowers remain native to floors 5–6.
- Boss opening indicators now have outlined text, a remaining-seconds label and a shrinking time bar. Cover collisions, avoided king combos and judgment recovery have distinct labels. No damage multiplier or opening-duration change is introduced.
- Fire timing applies to saved runs. Room compositions apply when rooms are generated (new expeditions or descent generation); saved room enemies are preserved.
